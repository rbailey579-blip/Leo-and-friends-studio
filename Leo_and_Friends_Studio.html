3. Create your silent animated video

Keep Safari open while rendering. Safari may save MP4 or WebM depending on iOS support. The video is silent; add voices in CapCut.

4. Finish in CapCut

  1. Open CapCut → New project → import your downloaded video from Files.
  2. Add the supplied narration script using Text-to-Speech if the feature is free in your account. Otherwise record the lines yourself.
  3. Import the SRT captions if supported, or add the captions as text.
  4. Add only music and sound effects you have permission to use.
  5. Export at 1080p and 30 fps, then watch the whole video before uploading.
